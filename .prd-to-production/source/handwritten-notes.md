## Finance

Problem — It's extremely complex, time-consuming, and tedious for students to manage their finances while trying to generate savings / achieve a greater ROI on asset investments.
1. There are too many different banks/apps with different interest rates with multiple tradeoffs to consider.
2. A ton of different asset classes and stock investments to choose from.
3. Multiple restaurant/food stalls with varying price ranges + menu items (decision fatigue).
4. Need to factor in budgeting for ingredients derived from recipes, indecision on whether to buy online/physical store for cheaper items.
5. High cognitive friction associated with deciding how to allocate a given sum budget across all these resources.

Solution:
1. An autonomous agent that helps you allocate your budget across all your expenses and savings effectively and efficiently for the next month.
   - Including the % to which bank product / stock asset
   - The restaurants and deals to use for that day
   - Taking into account transport fees
2. It autonomously rearranges and recreates the plan depending on new incoming expenses / budget changes.
